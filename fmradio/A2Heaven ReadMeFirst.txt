A2Heaven FM Radio New Desk Accessory		  (c) 2026 by Brutal Deluxe__________________________________________________________________Listen to your favorite FM stations from your Apple IIgs computer! The A2Heaven FM Radio card was developed by Plamen Vaysilov in 2026 for the Apple II line of computers. We wrote a New Desk Accessory to drive the FM Radio directly from your Apple IIgs.Installation Manual________________________________________________1/ Insert the FM Radio diskette in the Apple IIgs floppy drive.2/ Double-click the "Installer" program.3/ Install the "Desk Acc: a2Heaven FM Radio" extension script onto your boot disk.The following files are copied onto your startup disk:	- System:Desk.Accs:PlamenFMRadio	- System:Fonts:LED.244/ Restart your computer.Main window features________________________________________________	----------------------------------------	|  ---------       |------ (2) ------| |	|  |  (1)  |                           |	|  ---------       |------ (3) ------| |	|  | (8) |         |------ (4) ------| |	|  | (6) | | (7) | |------ (5) ------| |	----------------------------------------¥ (1) indicates the current channel frequency.¥ Use the pop-up menu (2) to switch between pre-programmed stations. You can also save a station, see below.¥ Buttons from (3) are:	¥ Soft-menu to get less noise,	¥ Mute to output... silence,	¥ Volume down or up.¥ Buttons from (4) allow you to:	¥ Tune down by frequency step set in (7),	¥ Seek down or up,	¥ Tune up.¥ Buttons from (5) set:	¥ Bass boost (yes, really),	¥ Mono/Stereo value,	¥ RDS on or off.You can store up to 8 preset stations in the Radio FM. When you click the "disk" button, the frequency is saved onto the card at the preset of the pop-up menu.¥ Pop-up Band (6) set the range of the minimum and maximum allowed frequencies found in the world.¥ Pop-up Spacing (7) allows you to adjust the frequency by an increment of 100, 200, 50, or 25 KhZ,¥ If the radio sends RDS data and the option is activated, it will be displayed in (8).When you close the New Desk Accessory, the FM Radio is not muted unless you press the Option key.Troubleshooting____________________________________________________¥ Application won't open	Check to be sure that the FM Radio is installed.¥ No sound from the IIgs	Plug external speakers.¥ Poor signal quality	Change the antenna's position.¥ Too few channels found	Change the antenna's position.Minimum system requirements_________________________________________The program requires at least 16KB of free memory.It has been tested on Apple IIgs ROM 01 & ROM 3 versions.It is compatible with System 6.x and above.Limited warranty___________________________________________________Many of the designations used by manufacturers and sellers to distinguish their products are claimed as trademarks.
