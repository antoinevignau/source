The Ocelot Cemetery

Explorer's Manual

THE STORY
The journey begins where maps no longer offer reassurance.

Deep within a forgotten valley lies a domain that travelers have avoided for generations. The ancients call it the Ocelot Cemetery: a territory of headstones, collapsed chapels, ravines, tunnels, and shrines, guarded by feline symbols and ancient mechanisms.

You are neither soldier nor conqueror. You enter as an explorer, guided by rumors, an incomplete map, and a long-told legend: at the heart of the Ocelot Cemetery lies a treasure chamber, concealed behind the domain's final gates.

The path leading there has been deliberately made difficult. Some passages are dangerous, certain gates remain shut, and many objects will only make sense when used in the right place. A plank might make a crossing safe, an inscription could reveal a clue, and a mechanism might open a previously unseen passage.

To reach the treasure chamber, you must observe every location, decipher the clues left by the ancients, gather the necessary items, and overcome the cemetery's various challenges.

Your goal is clear: traverse the domain, reach the Ocelot Cemetery, and discover the hidden treasure chamber.

- RESTART to start a new game (requires confirmation)
- QUIT to exit the game (requires confirmation)
- The LOAD and SAVE commands must be followed by a number (1 to 9) to work
